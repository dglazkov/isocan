# 25 October 2026 — every motion format, and a nightly red that was a missing login

**Motion phase 3** added WebM and GIF beside MP4 and the last three recipes
(`reveal`, `orbit`, `emphasis`). Both new encoders are our own (a ~130-line WebM
muxer, a ~270-line GIF encoder) rather than two more packages npm marks
deprecated; they live only in the render page's lazy chunk. GIF defaults to
30 fps, refuses above 50 (GIF delays are hundredths of a second) and refuses
past 64 MB on an estimate before any frame is drawn. Decoded WebM and GIF frames
each match their own pre-encode picture, and the web's path produces the CLI's
file. The phase stays PART-DONE until a person walks
[motion in the browser](../verify/2026-10-22-motion-in-the-browser.md): whether
motion *feels* right is the one thing no check here can say.
