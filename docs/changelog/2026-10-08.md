### web: a trackpad pinch zooms twice as far, once a frame

`9b05057`

Dion: zooming with the touchpad "feels slower than Figma... part of it is
how MUCH it zooms." Measured first, on a 200-item synthetic canvas in a
scratch daemon: a pinch event cost a median 0.7 ms with React's render
(p95 4.6, max 14), so on this machine the feel was the rate, not the work.

- The rate: exp(-deltaY × 0.011), twice 0.0055 and the rate tldraw and
  Excalidraw use. A pinch of 25 events of -4 now zooms ×3.00 (was ×1.73).
- One event's step is capped at 40 px: a ⌃-held mouse wheel sends ~100 a
  notch, which at the new rate would triple the zoom in one click. Firefox's
  line deltas (deltaMode 1) count 16 px each.
- Pinch events are gathered and applied once per frame at the latest
  cursor: a trackpad sends several a frame and 21 components read the
  viewport, so a heavy canvas paid that render several times a frame. In
  the browser, 25 events landed as one zoom in one frame.

pinchFactor is in lib/viewport.ts with its own test; Safari's gesture path
is unchanged (already 1:1 with the physical pinch).

Proof: npm run test:deep (807 files; the two export ratchets failed on a
test-only export and a misplaced comment, fixed, then pass); typecheck;
the bench canvas in the browser read scale 0.1597 → 0.4798 for one pinch,
unchanged within the frame it was sent in. Entry chunk 704,466.
