## A wash of the ground under the top controls

Asked for as: *Stitch has a white fade at the top when you scroll to the
top — add it, white on light and black on dark?* Built as the page's own
`--ground` token fading to nothing over the first 96px, which is white on
light and near-black on dark by construction rather than by two colours
kept in step. It sits over the items and under every piece of chrome, and
it is no pointer target, so a drag that starts under it still starts on the
canvas.

**It is chrome, so it can be turned off.** An entry in the hideable
registry (`canvas.topfade`) with the ⌘K action "Top fade" as its other door:
right-click the top bar brings it back like the rail brings History back,
Settings under your face has the switch, and "Show hidden controls" shows
everything. The guard that every hideable control names a door learned the
top edge as an area.
