# 5 September 2026 — The top fade

The night's module work is recorded on [4 September](2026-09-04.md), where
it began. This page starts with what came after it.

## A wash of the ground under the top controls

Asked for as: *Stitch has a white fade at the top when you scroll to the
top — add it, white on light and black on dark?* Built as the page's own
`--ground` token fading to nothing over the first 96px, which is white on
light and near-black on dark by construction rather than by two colours
kept in step. It sits over the items and under every piece of chrome, and
it is no pointer target, so a drag that starts under it still starts on the
canvas.

**It is chrome, so it can be turned off.** An entry in the hideable
registry (`canvas.topfade`) with the ⌘K action "Top fade" as its other door:
right-click the top bar brings it back like the rail brings History back,
Settings under your face has the switch, and "Show hidden controls" shows
everything. The guard that every hideable control names a door learned the
top edge as an area.

## Speaker notes, kept beside the slide

Asked for with one condition: *a way to show the speaker notes attached to
the slide on the canvas.* So a note is not a hidden field. It is a **text
item that points at its slide** (`noteFor=<slideId>` on an ordinary text
node), landing under the slide at the slide's width with a caption that
names the slide — visible where the deck is arranged, versioned, edited in
the stage or `$EDITOR`, draggable, a real `notes.md`. No new op, no new
kind, and the same item from both surfaces: the slide's right-click menu
(*Add speaker notes* / *Go to speaker notes*) and `isocan slides note
<slide> "what to say"`.

A note is never a slide, even when nothing is marked and the deck is every
item — a deck that projected its own notes is the one thing a presenter
cannot forgive. In full screen **N** shows the note under the stage, never
over it, and the browser remembers. The deck view's *With notes* prints
each note on its slide's sheet (`slides export deck.pdf --notes`), the
self-contained `deck.html` carries them and toggles on N, and `slides
export notes.md` writes the handout, saying which slides have nothing yet.

## Two small things from a screenshot

**The Add popover fits the window.** It hung off the top of the rail's +
button and grew downward; the + is the last thing on the rail, so on a
short window the list of canvases ran off the bottom and its last rows
could not be reached. It is placed against the viewport now — centred
beside the rail like the rail itself, never taller than the window, its
list scrolling inside it.

**Items that lead away say so.** A canvas card, a live site and a Google
Doc all read as pictures until you found the ↗. They wear a dashed border
in a token of their own now (`--away`, teal, so it is neither the accent,
the slide nor a warning) — dashed rather than a second solid hue because a
dashed edge reads as "through here", which is what the item is.

**The rail fits the window too.** The same short window lost both ends of
the tool rail: it was centred with a transform, which pushes a rail taller
than the window out at the top *and* the bottom, so the pointer and the +
were simply not there. It is centred by pinning both edges now, which
pins to the top when there is no room, and below 560px and 420px its
buttons shrink (32px, then 28px) so the whole rail stays on screen down to
a 340px window. Smaller buttons rather than a scrolling rail, because the
ink well and the Add popover hang off it and a scrolling rail would clip
them.

## Modules, phase 4: documents, and the three slots it asked for

The module the walk saved for last among the buildable ones, because it
needed slots nothing else had a use for. **Inspectors** mount beside the
workbench's stage for the open item's kind. **Pages** are cover routes of
their own at `/p/<canvas>/x/<segment>`, reachable from ⌘K and `isocan open
--page <segment>`; a segment nobody owns says so. **Commands** are slash
commands a module lays under the built-ins and the home's own, on both
surfaces, source `module`.

`packages/modules/documents` fills all three without adding a kind, a key
or an op: the **Outline** beside a document on the stage, the **Documents**
page listing every document newest first, `/outline` and `/summarize` for
an agent to carry out, `isocan docs ls` and `docs outline` to carry them
out with. The prose editor is deferred on purpose — a markdown round trip
through a rich editor is its own project — and the design says so.

Two loose ends with it: `module add` takes a git spec, a shallow clone in
front of the same code that reads a directory, proved against a local bare
repository; and the walk's position moves to "phase 5 waits on three named
gates".
