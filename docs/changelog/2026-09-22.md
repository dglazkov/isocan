# 22 September 2026 — Debts paid down: the entry chunk, main.ts, Node, and a queue emptied

A day of answering what the standing reviews had been saying for a week or
more, and of finding out that several reds were the tests' and not the
product's. Three pieces built in parallel — the entry-chunk reclaim, the
operator move, the summons receipt — were measured together at the end
(`113a667`): entry chunk 727,733, registry-lines 24,619 against its 25,267
bound, and **zero review findings unanswered**. (The 23 Sep page also touches
this groundwork; this is the day's own account.)

## The entry chunk loses 29 KB

The nightly read 756,004 bytes, 9,004 past the 747,000 ceiling, with the
findings of 21 and 22 Sep a day from reddening main (`d2f783f`). The emoji
picker (`core/emoji.ts` and `EmojiPicker.tsx`, ~25.7 KB) now loads behind the
`+`, warmed on pointer-enter or focus, and the Pen's colour names
(`core/colour.ts`, `core/contrast.ts`) arrive through a new
`@isocan/core/colour` subpath on the Pen's release — never the barrel, because
a dynamic `import("@isocan/core")` would pin all of core.

The lesson inside it: the lazy picker alone saved nothing.
`ALL_EMOJI = EMOJI_GROUPS.flatMap(...)` is a method call at module top level;
rollup cannot prove it free of side effects, so it kept the module wherever it
was statically reachable — through core's `export *`, every first visit.
`/* @__PURE__ */` on that one call is what let it split. A/B in one worktree:
756,060 → 726,962. The ceiling went *down* to 727,500, then 727,800 once the
three pieces were combined.

  object.

## A record reports; it never summons

A voice session posts its transcript to the Chat when it stops, and every
parked agent woke on it — a screenshot showed Scout starting to answer a
person's conversation with Enceladus (`f7f83e3`). `Comment.record` is the
distinction, and one clause at the top of `reasonFor` makes `wait`, the inbox
and the rc read one rule. It sits *before* the mention check on purpose:
transcripts are full of names, and "Scout should look at this" said aloud is
about Scout, not to Scout. Comment input may set it because it removes
authority rather than granting it; the model cannot. Lesson 91.
