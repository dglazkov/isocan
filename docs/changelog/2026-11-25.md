# 25 November 2026 — The Transition menu shows what each one does

"How can we quickly show what they may do?" A name like *Poof* says nothing
until you have seen it, and the menu's rows had only names and a tooltip.

Full screen's Transition menu now has a preview beside the list
(`TransitionPreview`): two miniature slides flipping forward and back on a
loop with whichever motion is under the pointer — or the keyboard, since the
menu now opens on the current choice with focus in the list and walks with
↑ ↓ Home End, Esc closing it — and the motion's one-line description.

It is the real choreography, not a drawing: the miniature is a whole
1280×800 slide scaled to 224×140, so the whole-slide motions wear the deck's
own keyframes (`.deck-moving > .deck-out/.deck-in`) and the content swaps and
the morph are planned by the same core functions the slide twins play
(`pieceSwapPlan`, `morphPlan`), over a scene with a footer in the same place
on both slides so the swaps can be seen leaving it pinned. Loaded the first
time the menu opens; still for somebody who asked for reduced motion.

Two fixes the preview needed in full screen: the chrome no longer rests while
a menu in the bar is open (somebody holding still to watch a preview is using
it — measured: the menu faded out from under the pointer after 2.5s), and an
open menu keeps its own keys, so ↓ in the list moves through it rather than
flipping a slide in the deck underneath.
