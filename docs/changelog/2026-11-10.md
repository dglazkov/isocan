## Links out of an item open a new tab

A deck slide's `tabletop.google ↗` pill did nothing in full screen: item frames
were sandboxed without `allow-popups`, so the browser dropped every
`target="_blank"` click. Content-origin item frames now also carry
`allow-popups allow-popups-to-escape-sandbox` (`ITEM_FRAME_LINKS` in
`packages/web/src/lib/frame.ts`), so a click opens an ordinary tab on the
target's own origin. Nothing gains `allow-top-navigation`, so a page still cannot
replace the canvas tab, and a home with no content origin keeps today's frame
byte for byte — its app-origin `sandbox allow-scripts` header would veto popups
anyway. `contentorigin.test.ts` holds all three, and the agent guide's items
topic has a short *Links out of an HTML item*.

## Every layer walked in a browser, and what had slipped past the CSS scale

(Lesson 128.)

`packages/web/test/layers.test.ts` held the `:root` z-index scale and caught raw
numbers in stylesheets, and four real classes of layer defect still shipped past
it because none of them looks wrong inside a single CSS rule:

1. **Containing-block traps (`backdrop-filter` and scroll boxes)** — when
   floating chrome took `--glass-filter` (`backdrop-filter: blur(6px)
   saturate(1.45)`), every `position: fixed` descendant inside a glass surface
   started measuring its offsets against that chrome slab instead of the
   viewport, inherited its stacking context (`--z-float: 26` < `--z-bar: 30`),
   and clipped when the slab scrolled. `AddPopover` portalled its `<form>` to
   `document.body` (`AddPopover.tsx`, `add-popover.css`), and `CanvasTools.tsx`
   restored the rail's eager `+` `Add` button (`aria-label="Add"`), which had
   been hidden whenever `adding` was `null` since `AddPopover` went lazy on 13
   Sep (`902dab77a`). `AgentRow.tsx` portalled all three `.wb-peek` trail cards
   to `document.body` so neither `.dock-panel` (`backdrop-filter` + `overflow:
   hidden`) nor `.workbench`'s scroll columns clip them. And on phones
   (`max-width: 480px`), `.bar-cluster .identity-popover` escapes `position:
   fixed` trapping inside `.bar-cluster.floats` by anchoring `position:
   absolute` to the cluster's right edge at `calc(100vw - 2 * var(--edge))`.
2. **Portalled transient UI behind full-screen covers** — `.workbench`,
   `.fullscreen`, and `.module-page` cover the window at `--z-fullscreen` (55),
   while body-portalled menus, peeks and toasts (`.mention-menu`,
   `.hover-card`, `.wb-peek`, `.context-menu`, `.proposal-toast`) sat at
   `--z-popover` (40) or `--z-toast` (45) — invisible behind the cover whenever
   typing `@` or `/`, right-clicking, or hovering an agent or bound file row in
   the Workbench or Stage Focus. `body:has(.workbench/.fullscreen/.module-page)`
   now lifts those transient surfaces to `--z-modal` (90), strictly below
   dialogs (`--z-dialog`: 100).
3. **Top History scrubber tooltip off-screen** — when History moved to the top
   under a bottom Chat bar (`.scrubber.at-top`), `.scrub-peek` kept `bottom:
   calc(100% + 10px)` and rendered at `y = -10` off the top of the viewport.
   `.scrubber.at-top .scrub-peek` now opens downward (`top: calc(100% + 10px)`),
   and `.scrubber.at-top` steps clear of `.follow-banner` and `.sprint-chip`.
4. **Co-resident chrome collisions** — with Chat docked at the bottom,
   `width: min(720px, calc(100vw - 440px))` keeps `.chat-bar` side-by-side
   between `.minimap-panel` and `.zoom-controls` down to `760px` (stepping the
   corner controls above full-width `.chat-bar` below `760px`). Whenever bottom
   centre is occupied by `.chat-bar` or a bottom `.scrubber`, the wet-ink
   `.sketch-bar` pill moves to top centre (`top: calc(var(--topbar) + 12px)`).
   With a left dock open, bottom `.scrubber` centres in the remaining canvas
   stage while `.minimap-dock` steps cleanly above it (`bottom: 154px`), and
   below `800px` (`460px + 340px` left-dock shift) an open-dock `.minimap-dock`
   lifts above `.zoom-controls` just as a closed-dock map does below `460px`.
   On the right edge, `.trash-panel` joins `.marks` as a floating card
   (`right: 76px; bottom: calc(var(--edge) + 53px)`, with
   `TRASH_WIDTH + MARKS_GUTTER` matching in `stage.ts`), and `.explain-panel`,
   `.walk-panel` (clearing both rails and the `168px` minimap), `.away-panel`,
   `.module-overlays-left/right`, and `.canvas-group-scope` clear the top bar,
   side rails, open left dock, and zoom bar. `CursorLayer.tsx` also drops its
   inline `zIndex: 25` for `var(--z-canvas)`.
