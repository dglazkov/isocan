- **AGENTS.md gains "Done means done on both surfaces"**, and `packages/cli/test/surface.test.ts` enforces the part that can be enforced. It found two undocumented commands on its first run.

## Watching, and working notes

- `isocan wait --item --op`: a watcher sleeps through everything except the change it waits for. A summons always gets through; your own ops never wake you.
- **`comment.update`** — an agent can say "on it" and keep that one message current. Comments carry `editedAt`, so the canvas renders "edited · 4m" from its own clock rather than an agent's estimate.
