# 17 October 2026 — Mood board from exploration to the Board critic, and durable agents specified

## Why a design system needed a feeling before a button

Starting a design system from tokens and font names asks a person to decide the smallest things first. When the canvas starts empty, the hard question is what the product should *feel* like — light, material, texture, colour, era, density, place, and lettering — before anyone draws a button or names a typeface. And once a person reacts to pictures and keeps the ones that fit, those kept pictures should stay at work: first as deterministic colour swatches the CLI can read without decoding images, then as the cited source of every palette token and direction choice in `DESIGN.md`, and finally as a critic on the canvas that checks whether later screens still look like what was kept.

## What landed (`docs/projects/moodboard/`, phases 1–4)

- **Phase 1 — Keep, drop, more, and push across CLI and web (`packages/core/src/mood.ts`, `packages/modules/mood/src/cli.ts`, `packages/modules/mood/src/web.tsx`, `server/model-routes.ts`).**
  Added shared board and tray mechanics (`findMoodBoard`, `moodKeepPlan`, `moodDropOps`, `moodTrays`, `MOOD_DIALS`, `moodPushPrompt`) for both legacy (`kind: "area"`) and group-mode (`group.change` / `containerId`) canvases. `isocan mood keep` moves candidate tiles into the **Mood** board (creating it once and reusing it) and refuses agent actors (`isAgentSession`) so taste is never delegated by proxy; `isocan mood drop` deletes candidates by id or `--run` in one undoable group; `isocan mood more` draws four variations from any tile with optional `--push <dial>` (`warmer`, `cooler`, `quieter`, `louder`, `older`, `newer`, `rougher`, `finer`). Behind the `moodboard` experiment, `@isocan/mood/web` adds `/moodboard`, **Explore a look…** in Design systems, the candidate tray overlay (per-tile and multi-select keep/drop, **More like this**, **Push →**, and **Close tray**), item menu actions on kept and reference tiles, and `mood: "reference"` on photos dropped onto the Mood board.
- **Phase 2 — Deterministic CIE L\*a\*b\* k-means swatches (`packages/core/src/mood-swatches.ts`, `packages/server/src/mood-swatches.ts`, `packages/modules/mood/src/web.tsx`).**
  Added seeded CIE $L^*a^*b^*$ k-means extraction (`swatchesFromRgba`, `deltaE`, `rgbToLab`, `MOOD_SWATCH_MAX_EDGE = 128`, merging clusters within $\Delta E < 8$ and dropping shares $< 0.04$). In the browser, keeping a candidate tile or dropping a photo onto the Mood board extracts 3–6 swatches client-side and writes `moodSwatches` alongside `mood = "kept" | "reference"`. On the daemon, `POST /api/image/swatches` decodes the canvas blob via `sharp` when installed or falls back to a picture-capable text model marked `estimated: true` (refusing with `503 image-swatches-unavailable` when neither is available), so `isocan mood keep` writes the same `moodSwatches` with no browser open. `@isocan/mood/web` claims image items carrying `moodSwatches` (`property: "moodSwatches"`) and renders `.mood-swatch-strip` under each tile without adding a byte to the entry chunk. Added `WebModule.fileProperties` (`packages/core/src/modules.ts`, `packages/web/src/modules.ts`) so native file drops onto the Mood board stay inside `@isocan/mood/web` and `packages/web/src/lib/upload.ts` never names `@isocan/mood`.
- **Phase 3 — Deriving `DESIGN.md` with board tile citations (`packages/core/src/mood.ts`, `packages/core/src/designcheck.ts`, `packages/core/src/command-bodies.ts`, `DesignSystemsDialog.tsx`, `DesignSystemView.tsx`).**
  Added `moodBoardRead` and `isocan mood board [--json]` to read kept and reference tiles in reading order with their `axis`, `prompt`, `words`, and `swatches`. Updated `/design-system`'s command body so agents derive tokens from `isocan mood board --json` and record every palette token (`colors.<role>`) and direction field (`direction.<field>`, `direction.treatments.<Name>`) in `tokens.isocan.sources`. Extended `readDesignSources` and `checkDesign(doc, { boardTileIds })` (wired into `isocan style check`) so any uncited palette colour or direction field not explained in `direction.rationale`, or any source citing an item not on the Mood board, fails the check while `isocan design audit` runs unchanged. In the web app, **Design systems** gains **Derive from the board** (dispatching `@<lead> /design-system` with `[boardId]` or showing a one-sentence notice when no agent is parked) and renders source tile chips; hovering or focusing a cited token in `DesignSystemsDialog` or `DesignSystemView` calls `setPeeked(itemId)` to light its source tile on the canvas.
- **Phase 4 — The board as a built-in critic (`packages/core/src/mood.ts`, `packages/modules/critique/src/cli.ts`, `room-cli.ts`, `web.tsx`, `room-web.tsx`).**
  Added `boardCriticDraft`, `findOffBoardColours`, and `boardCritiqueNotes` in `@isocan/core/mood` (imported directly from `@isocan/core/mood` so `@isocan/critique` never names `@isocan/mood` and the web entry chunk stays untouched). When tiles sit on the Mood board, `boardCriticDraft` builds a real critique persona card (`name: "board"`, `agentName: "Board"`, `critique.critic = "board"`) quoting the kept tiles' titles, axes, words, and hex swatches. On the CLI, `isocan critic ls` offers `board` under starter lenses, `isocan critic add board` places the card, and `isocan critique <item> --by board` (and `--as board`) places the card on the fly before asking. On the web, **Critique with…** offers a **Board** row and **Critics in the room** offers a **Board** button under **Add a lens**. Proved across unit, CLI, and 11 headless Chrome checks in `scripts/journey-moodboard.mjs` (Scene 4 pins a `working` note on kept crust `#b5651d` and a `risk` note naming off-board blue `#2563eb` on `Acme Checkout`). A human walk with a paid Gemini key is queued in `docs/verify/2026-10-17-moodboard.md`.
