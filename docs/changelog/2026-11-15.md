copied by hand, so the module should lift one app's design system onto the
canvas and offer it to another. Then built (`97a6a06ca`, part-done):

## A flaky setup test, made reliable

`setup-npx.test.ts` picked its daemon port by listening on 0 and closing, so
another test worker's daemon could take the number back before setup probed
it; setup then adopted that daemon and never wrote its "stay on this machine"
reason, and cleanup killed the other worker. Both ports now come from
`reservePort()`, as `test/ports.ts` asks (`eb8f14405`). No product change.
