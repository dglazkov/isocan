# The operator, on the hosted home

**What you need:** everything listed under *People and surfaces* in
[the operator runbook](../projects/operator/acceptance.md) — chiefly three
identities (an operator, an owner who is not one, a link-only stranger), two
devices for the owner, and two networks. About two hours.

**Why this page exists.** The operator can see a canvas somebody reported,
take it down, erase it, end an account, revoke a link and refuse a network —
and every one of those passed against a local home and the emulator. None has
been done on a real hosted home, by a person proving who they are with a real
emailed sign-in link. Until that happens, answering a real takedown request
would be the first test.

**This page is the doorway, not a copy.** The steps are the runbook's, in its
order; it was written by whoever built the acts and should not be restated
here.

---

1. Before anything: a person confirms dev is running the build you think it
   is, and that the operator address is on its list. The runbook says what to
   record.
2. Walk [the runbook](../projects/operator/acceptance.md) sections 1 to 6 on
   **disposable, made-up canvases only** — never a real one.
3. At each section, check the ledger as its text says: **every act should
   have a row before it answered**, including refusals.

## Writing it down

The runbook's *Record and close* section says what to keep and what never to
paste (no tokens, links or cookies). Then set this page's front matter to
`works`, or `broken` with an issue, and move the operator phases it closes.
Production comes after dev, as a second run of the same walk.
