(`relay.localhost`, `[::1]:8453`, a local CA, account `acme`) · `bun exact.mjs web --relay relay.localhost:8453

`--host-resolver-rules` sending `*.relay.localhost` to the relay and the loop's key pinned.

## Rough

- The canvas gateway breaks this app's page. Its loopback rewriter reads minified objects such as `{0:0}` and
  `{0:1}` in `app.js` as a bare `host:port` (`0` parses as 0.0.0.0) and writes `{loopback.invalid}`: a
  SyntaxError, and the page is blank through the relay. Direct on the loop it renders. Reproduced by fetching
  `/.exact/gen/<gen>/app.js` both ways and `cmp`: first difference at line 2, column 34947.
- The relay's canvas is framed by its own tool's origin only (`frame-ancestors 'self' https://<tool>`, every
  answer and the entry page), so no other canvas can frame a teammate's live page. A third-party canvas needs an
  ancestor it can name, e.g. `--canvas-ancestor <origin>` on `web --relay`.
- The relay door refuses every `Origin` but its tool's (403). The only way another page's code reaches the wire
  is `--allow-plugin-origin` and a sandboxed (`Origin: null`) frame — which works, but reads as a Figma
  allowance, not a canvas one.
- `limits.perIpPerSession` (30 a minute) is less than one page load through the canvas gateway: ~25 requests,
  each on its own connection. The page's modules failed `ERR_CONNECTION_CLOSED` until it was raised to 600.
- `/__dev/page` through the gateway is cut after ~10 s idle (`ERR_INCOMPLETE_CHUNKED_ENCODING`); the loop
  itself keeps it open.

## Lean in

- `pair-code` with `scope: "read"` minted by the local token, entered through the relay, and the wire's refusals
  name both scopes: `scrub needs \`write\` scope; this client has \`read\``, `propose needs \`propose\` scope`.
  Nothing on isocan's side decides a scope; it quotes the wire.
- The relay passes TLS through: the teammate's certificate is the loop's own wildcard (its key pin printed at
  start), so the relay never sees a token or a design.
- `--relay-cert/--relay-key` and `NODE_EXTRA_CA_CERTS` made a loopback relay a faithful stand-in with no ACME.

## Needed

- The gateway's rewriter must not touch `<digits>:<digits>` inside JS objects (or skip JS bodies it cannot parse).
- A canvas ancestor of the developer's choosing for the relay canvas.
- A documented way for a third-party page to call the relay wire other than the plugin allowance.

## Checkpoints

- Teammate paired `read` through the relay; drag refused 403 on the wire; canvas proposal held at `read` refused
  403; app files hashed equal before and after.
- Live page through the relay: loaded over HTTPS at the canvas name, blank (the rewriter above).
- Viewer without a pairing: captures only, one sentence on the frame.
