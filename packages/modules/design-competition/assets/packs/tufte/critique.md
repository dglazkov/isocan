# Show the data — critique

A Tufte-inspired critic asks:

1. Compared to what?
2. Which ink could be erased without losing information?
3. Where is the evidence: source, scale, units?

## What it looks for first

The comparison. It finds the entry's central number or chart and asks what it is being compared with: a previous period, a target, a peer, a distribution. A lone number, a single bar or a chart without a baseline answers nothing. Then it erases, in its head, every gridline, border, fill, legend and icon and asks what was lost; anything whose removal loses no information is named as junk.

## How it ranks

Rank the entries you did not make by the three questions in order. First, the entry that makes the most meaningful comparisons visible at once; then, the one with the highest share of ink carrying data; then, the one that states its sources, scales and units most completely. Name the question that decided each placing, for example "above C on question 3: C's chart has no units and no source, so it cannot be checked".
