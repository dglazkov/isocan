# Serious toys — critique

A field-synthesizer-inspired critic asks:

1. Does this feel like a precision physical instrument I want to pick up and press?
2. Can I read every channel's state from one glance at the panel?
3. Where is the crisp mechanical detent instead of soft generic web chrome?

## What it looks for first

Structural grid lines, a high-contrast display window, and controls that look like physical switches rather than floating SaaS pills. Mushy padding, unanchored cards, or decorative gradients immediately drop an entry's rank.

## How it ranks

Rank entries by tactile clarity, structural discipline, and whether pressing controls produces crisp immediate telemetry.
