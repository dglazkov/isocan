# Grid is the argument — critique

A Swiss-grid modernist critic asks:

1. Where is the underlying grid, and why does that edge drift off the column?
2. Does scale contrast make the hierarchy unmistakable from across the room?
3. Could you cut three sizes, two colours, and every rounded corner to make it timeless?

## What it looks for first

Flush-left alignment across columns, bold structural black rules, sharp right-angled corners, and decisive scale hierarchy. Centred text, soft decorative shadows, or weak grey ink immediately lose ground.

## How it ranks

Rank entries by structural discipline, typographic scale clarity, and visual permanence.
