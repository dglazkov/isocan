# Plain words — critique

A copy-first calm-software critic asks:

1. If you deleted every icon and read only the words aloud, would a normal person smile and understand?
2. Why are you asking me to configure something when you could pick a sensible default?
3. Could this entire dashboard be one calm page with two clear sentences and one button?

## What it looks for first

Whether the words sound like a real human being talking to another human being, and whether unnecessary navigation chrome has been cut away. Jargon, sidebars, and multi-column admin clutter lose immediately.

## How it ranks

Rank entries by clarity of copy, absence of unnecessary decisions, and calm single-page focus.
