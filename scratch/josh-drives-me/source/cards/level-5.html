Level 5 of 8 · highway

🛣️ Merge MasterWeeks 10–12 · Nov 16 – Dec 6 · 12 sessions · 4 at night

Where: Highway: first on a Sunday at 7 am, then busier, then at night
Why now: A merge asks for speed control, blind-spot checks, lane changes and gap choice all at once. Every one of those is now stable. Time.

The sessions (30 min each)
  • S1Prep at 45 mph on a divided road: speed matching, mirror rhythm every 5–8 s, holding a lane at speed.
  • S2FIRST MERGE: Sunday 7 am, one easy on-ramp, one exit. Accelerate to traffic speed IN the ramp. Repeat ×3.
  • S320 minutes on the highway: lane discipline, 4-second gap, exactly the limit.
  • S4Lane changes at speed ×10: pass on the left, return right, mirror-signal-shoulder every time.
  • S5Exits: slow in the ramp, not on the highway. Read ramp speed signs; curves tighten.
  • S6 🌙NIGHT: highway after dark — high/low beams, trucks, judging closing speed by headlights.
  • S7Moderate weekday traffic (mid-morning).
  • S8Interchanges: left exits, weave lanes, express lanes, ‘keep right except to pass’.
  • S9 🌙NIGHT: night highway with two exits and a re-entry.
  • S10Heavy traffic: stop-and-go at 15 mph, 2-second cushion, eyes in the mirror for the car behind.
  • S11 🌙NIGHT: an unfamiliar highway route with voice navigation.
  • S12 🌙NIGHT · BOSS RUN: the 25-minute highway loop.
Kitt watches for
⚠️ Merging below traffic speed⚠️ Stopping at the end of the ramp⚠️ Braking on the highway for an exit⚠️ Camping in the left lane
Coach's tip. Choose the ramp for the lesson: long acceleration lane, good sightlines, light traffic, dry daylight, and an exit soon after. Never the first merge at rush hour.
🎮 Boss battle — pass to unlock Level 625-minute highway loop in moderate traffic: 4 merges, 6 lane changes, 2 exits, cushion never under 3 seconds.
