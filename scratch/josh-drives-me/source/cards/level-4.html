  • S8 🌙NIGHT: wet roads if you get them, otherwise night arterials with lane changes.

  • S11Josh picks the route and the goal. Kitt speaks only for safety.
  • S12 🌙NIGHT · BOSS RUN: 30-minute urban loop at dusk.
