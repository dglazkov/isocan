Level 2 of 8 · traffic lights

🚦 Lights & LanesWeeks 3–4 · Sep 28 – Oct 11 · 8 sessions

Where: 35–45 mph arterial roads, quiet hours first
Why now: First traffic lights and first real lane changes. Nearly every test route is made of exactly this.

The sessions (30 min each)
  • S1First lights at a quiet hour: stale green vs fresh green, the yellow decision point, where to stop, right-on-red = full stop + yield.
  • S2Left turns at lights: protected arrow vs unprotected. Creep into the intersection, wheels STRAIGHT while waiting, pick gaps.
  • S3Lane changes ×20 on a wide, empty arterial using SMOG: Signal · Mirror · Over-the-shoulder · Go.
  • S4Following distance 3–4 s, scanning 12–15 s ahead, covering the brake near driveways and crosswalks.
  • S5School zones, crosswalks, cyclists, bus stops, speed-limit changes. Say every sign out loud.
  • S6Double-lane turns into the correct lane, center turn lanes, first one-way street.
  • S7Moderate traffic (after-school hour): put it all together for 22 minutes.
  • S8BOSS RUN: 10 lane changes with full SMOG + 5 unprotected lefts + 5 lights, narrated.
Kitt watches for
⚠️ Signal after braking, not before⚠️ Drifting wide on lefts⚠️ Speeding up for yellows⚠️ Skipped shoulder checks
Coach's tip. Say the decision early and calmly: “in 200 feet we'll turn right” beats “TURN HERE”. An instruction shouted late is a critical error you caused.
🎮 Boss battle — pass to unlock Level 310 lane changes (every one with a shoulder check), 5 unprotected lefts, 5 lights, ending in the correct lane every time.
