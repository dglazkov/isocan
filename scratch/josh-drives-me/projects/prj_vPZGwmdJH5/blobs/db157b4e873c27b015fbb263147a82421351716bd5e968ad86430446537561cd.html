Level 1 of 8 · precision

🏠 Home Turf, UpgradedWeeks 1–2 · Sep 14 – Sep 27 · 8 sessions

Where: Your neighborhood + one empty parking lot
Why now: Josh is comfortable here. Comfort is the raw material; this level turns it into precision the examiner can see.

The sessions (30 min each)
  • S1Baseline lap. Seat, mirrors, 9-and-3 hands. Kitt narrates the drive (commentary driving), then Josh takes over the narration.
  • S2Stops & signals: full stop behind the line every time, signal 100 ft early, turn into the correct lane, head check on every turn.
  • S3Backing: straight back 100 ft looking through the rear window, then backing around a corner. Camera is a helper, not the view.
  • S4Three-point turn ×10 on a quiet street. Signal, mirror, shoulder, turn, back, go.
  • S5Empty lot: perpendicular parking forward, then reverse-in. Learn reference points on Josh's car (mirror-to-line, etc.).
  • S6Hill parking (up/down, curb/no curb), U-turns, and speed control at 15 / 25 mph without looking at the speedometer.
  • S7Lot drills: hard ABS stop from 25 mph, blind-spot mapping with a parked car, hand-over-hand vs push-pull.
  • S8BOSS RUN: three-point turn + reverse into the driveway + 5 full stops, zero corrections from Kitt.
Kitt watches for
⚠️ Rolling stops⚠️ Looking at the hood, not 12 s ahead⚠️ Camera-only backing⚠️ Hands at 10-and-2 or one-handed
Coach's tip. Commentary driving is the engine of this whole plan: Josh says out loud what he sees and what he'll do. Model it first for 5 minutes, then hand it over.
