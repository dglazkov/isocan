Level 3 of 8 · parking + roundabouts

🅿️ Parallel UniverseWeeks 5–6 · Oct 12 – Oct 25 · 8 sessions · 2 at night

Where: Cones on a quiet street, real cars, busy lots, roundabouts. First night drives.
Why now: Parallel parking is where Colorado test-takers burn their one allowed pull-up. Roundabouts are everywhere on the Front Range.

The sessions (30 min each)
  • S1Parallel park with cones, reference-point method, ×8. Slow is smooth. (Kitt: set the cones 25 ft apart to start, then 22.)
  • S2Parallel ×10 between real cars on a quiet street, both left and right sides. Finish within 18 in of the curb.
  • S3Busy lot: pedestrians, backing out with a 360° look, angled spaces, a drive-through.
  • S4Single-lane roundabouts: slow to 15, yield to traffic in the circle, signal the exit. ×10 laps.
  • S5Multi-lane roundabouts (pick the lane before you enter) + a tight parking garage.
  • S6 🌙NIGHT 1: neighborhood + arterials after dark. Headlights on, dash dim, glare, speed feels slower at night.
  • S7 🌙NIGHT 2: night parking lot + 3 parallel parks under streetlights.
  • S8BOSS RUN: 3 of 3 parallel parks with no pull-up, then reverse-in ×3.
Kitt watches for
⚠️ Curb strike (a critical error on the test)⚠️ Stopping inside a roundabout⚠️ Not signaling the roundabout exit⚠️ Forgetting headlights at dusk
Coach's tip. Reference points beat “feel”: when the back of the front car lines up with your mirror, full lock. Write Josh's points on a sticky note in the car.
