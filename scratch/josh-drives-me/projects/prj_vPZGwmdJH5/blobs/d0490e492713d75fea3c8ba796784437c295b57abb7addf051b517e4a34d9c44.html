Level 6 of 8 · Colorado edition

🏔️ Weather & WildWeeks 13–15 · Dec 7 – Dec 27 · 12 sessions · 4 at night

Where: Snowy lots, canyon roads, dusk wildlife hours, sun glare. Timed for December on purpose.
Why now: Colorado will hand Josh snow, ice, grades, deer and blinding sun in his first year. Better with Kitt in the seat than alone.

The sessions (30 min each)
  • S1Empty snowy (or wet) lot: ABS stops, feeling the skid start, gentle inputs, steering into the slide.
  • S2Snow roads: double the following distance, no cruise control, brake early and straight.
  • S3Ice awareness: bridges and shade first, black ice, why you scrape the WHOLE windshield.
  • S4Mountain road: downhill in a low gear, brake fade, curves, pull-outs for faster traffic.
  • S5 🌙NIGHT: wildlife hour at dusk — scanning the ditches, high-beam etiquette, don't swerve for small animals.
  • S6Sun glare at sunrise/sunset: visor, sunglasses, following the lane line, slowing down.
  • S7Gravel / dirt road: loose surface, washboard, dust from oncoming cars.
  • S8 🌙NIGHT: snow at night if you have it, otherwise the coldest, darkest arterial run you can find.
  • S9Traction & chain laws (read the signs), then a foothills highway with grades and trucks.
  • S10 🌙NIGHT: a mountain road after dark.
  • S11Breakdown drill: pull over fully, hazards, what's in the trunk kit, and the after-a-crash checklist.
  • S12 🌙NIGHT · BOSS RUN: Kitt's hard drive.
Kitt watches for
⚠️ Cruise control in snow⚠️ Riding the brakes downhill⚠️ Swerving for wildlife⚠️ Peephole-scraped windshield
